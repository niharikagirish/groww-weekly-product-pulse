IDENTIFIED FEE-RELATED ISSUE
Multiple reviews report confusion and perceived high or duplicate charges; examples mention "₹20 per order", "brokerage charged twice", and "very high charges & taxes". These reports indicate perceived lack of fee transparency but do not include transaction receipts to verify errors.

VERIFIED FEE EXPLANATION
- Groww charges brokerage for executing trades (verified policy).
- Equity trading fee is either ₹20 or 0.1% of trade value, whichever is lower.
- Minimum equity charge is ₹5.
- SEBI regulatory cap: brokerage for equity delivery trades cannot exceed 2.5% of trade value.
- F&O trading carries a flat fee of ₹20 per order.
- Regulatory/statutory/exchange fees and penalties are imposed by exchanges/government and are not charged by Groww; customer allegations of duplicate or unexplained charges are not verified by the official policy.
